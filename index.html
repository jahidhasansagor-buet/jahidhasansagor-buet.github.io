At a glance

Mechanical engineer with 5+ years of combined experience in thermal research and HVAC systems. My doctoral work showed that using room-temperature material properties can underpredict thermal conductance by 32–43% at 1200 K — results published in Physical Review B. Before my Ph.D., I spent two years running chillers, AHUs, and BMS in a GMP pharmaceutical plant, including commissioning a 600-ton water-cooled chiller from specification through performance testing.
