About me

I work at the intersection of heat transfer, computational modeling, and thermal systems engineering. My doctoral research uses first-principles calculations and thermal-transport models to study how material behavior changes heat transfer at very small length scales. Alongside this research, I have experience with CFD, thermodynamic analysis, and large-scale numerical simulation. Before graduate school, I worked in HVAC and utility operations, including chiller commissioning, AHU and BMS operation, troubleshooting, and team supervision in a pharmaceutical manufacturing environment.
